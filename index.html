How it works

Agents that work like teammates.

Each agent has a name, a job, its own memory and its own folder. You see what they do as they do it.

A team, not a chatbot

Put agents in a group and talk to the whole team. Frog, the lead agent, splits the work and hands each part to the right teammate.

  • Live status shows who is working on what
  • Hand-offs between agents appear right in the chat
  • Type @Name to talk to one agent

Nothing happens without you

Anything that sends, posts, deletes or runs a command asks first. If an agent needs you in another chat, a bar at the top shows it, with the buttons right there.

  • Allow, Don't allow, or always allow
  • Stop any agent at any time

Ask first, Auto, or Plan

Choose how much freedom each agent or group has. In Plan mode the agent only plans; when you like the plan, one click runs it.

  • Switch with one click or Shift+Tab
  • Set per agent and per group

Every action on the record

The Logs view lists everything your team did, with date and time. Filter it, copy it, or save it.

Features

Everything a small team needs.

Agents with real jobs

Describe the job in plain words. Each agent keeps its own memory and files.

Groups and mentions

Agents message each other and can ask you to add a teammate when they need one.

Its own browser

Each agent drives a private Chrome profile: opens sites, reads pages, fills forms when you allow it.

Connects to your apps

Slack, Notion, GitHub, Linear, or any MCP server. Slack setup starts from a single button.

Spends fewer tokens

App tools load only when needed and old output is trimmed, so you pay less for AI.

Keeps working

Close the window and your team carries on from the tray while your computer is on.

Privacy

Local by design.

Frog Bot runs on your computer and keeps your team there. Here is exactly what stays and what leaves.

Stays on your computer

  • Agents, chats and memory, in one folder called FrogsWorld
  • Files your agents create and share
  • Your API key and app sign-ins, encrypted on your machine
  • Logs of everything your team did

Leaves only when needed

  • Messages to the AI model you choose (Claude or OpenAI), with your own key
  • Requests to apps you connect and sites agents visit, for the work you give them
  • Your name and email, so you can sign in to your account

Questions

What do I need?

A computer running Windows 10 or 11, macOS, or 64-bit Linux, and an API key from Anthropic (console.anthropic.com) or OpenAI (platform.openai.com). You pay the AI provider directly for what your agents use.

Does anything leave my computer?

Your agents, chats, files, memory, logs and encrypted keys stay in a folder called FrogsWorld on your computer. To think, an agent sends its messages to the AI model you chose. Agents also reach the apps you connect and the websites they visit, only for the work you give them. Your name and email are kept with your account so you can sign in.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one. Create it in the app with your name, email and a password of at least 6 characters. Each account has its own private team on your computer.

Is it really free?

Yes. Frog Bot is open source under the MIT license. The only cost is what your AI provider charges for your key.

Mac says the app can't be opened or is damaged

Frog Bot is not signed with an Apple certificate yet. The one-line installer fixes this for you. If you downloaded the .zip, run this once in Terminal: xattr -cr "/Applications/Frog Bot.app" && codesign --force --deep --sign - "/Applications/Frog Bot.app"

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. Windows shows this for new apps that are not code-signed yet.

Where are my files?

In a folder called FrogsWorld in your home folder: each agent's files, shared files, logs and settings.
